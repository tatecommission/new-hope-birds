HABITAT DATA

Six CSVs in ../habitat_data, one per location and season. Each contains six
habitat rows, with the original survey years as columns. Values are sums of
available numeric bird counts, not species richness or effort-adjusted estimates.

chapel_hill_spring_habitat.csv: 1952-2024, 68 surveyed years.
chapel_hill_winter_habitat.csv: 1923-2023, 93 surveyed years.
durham_spring_habitat.csv: 1974-2022, 49 surveyed years.
durham_winter_habitat.csv: 1968-2023, 55 surveyed years (1972 absent).
jordan_lake_spring_habitat.csv: 1977-2022, 46 surveyed years.
jordan_lake_winter_habitat.csv: 1977-2021, 45 surveyed years.
Jordan Lake winter preserves its source's start-year convention: 2021 is 2021-22.
No missing years are interpolated or inserted.

REBUILD IN R
From the project root, run:
  Rscript r-files/build-habitat-data.R
Dependency: jsonlite. Install once if necessary: install.packages("jsonlite").
The R script performs source import, annual count extraction, classification,
aggregation, reconciliation, CSV export, and saved-file validation. It reads
habitat_species_mapping.json for assignments, aliases, and source citations.
All original input files remain unchanged.

HABITAT GROUPS
Forest birds: Eastern Forest, Western Forest, Boreal Forest, Forest Generalist.
Grassland birds: Grassland.
Aridland birds: Aridlands.
Wetland and coastal birds: Wetland and Coasts.
Tundra birds: Arctic Tundra.
Habitat generalists: Habitat Generalist.

Assignments describe primary breeding habitat, even for local winter counts.
The principal reference is Rosenberg et al. (2019), Data S1, reproduced in the
Cornell-hosted paper and supplement:
https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aaw1313
https://www.birds.cornell.edu/home/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/DECLINE-OF-NORTH-AMERICAN-AVIFAUNA-SCIENCE-2019.pdf

All named species are assigned: 276 distinct named species and two hybrid labels.
The mapping retains original source labels and standardized names. Explicit
aliases handle capitalization, punctuation, spelling, obsolete names, and
subspecies labels. Alias rows are combined across years; overlapping positive
counts for aliases of the same species/year cause an error rather than being
silently double-counted.

SUPPLEMENTARY ASSIGNMENTS
The study labels Mute Swan, European Starling, House Sparrow, and Rock Pigeon
as Introduced instead of assigning one of the ten breeding biomes. Project
assignments place Mute Swan in wetland/coastal and the other three in habitat
generalists, including birds of human-modified environments.
Iceland Gull and Lesser Black-backed Gull, absent from Data S1, are assigned to
wetland/coastal based on coastal breeding habitats. Red Phalarope, also absent
from Data S1, is assigned to tundra based on its breeding habitat. These seven
choices use Cornell All About Birds accounts linked in the mapping; they are
project assignments rather than published Data S1 classifications.
Brewster's Warbler is assigned to forest because both parental species are
Eastern Forest birds. The White-throated Sparrow x Dark-eyed Junco hybrid is
also assigned to forest because both parents belong to that group in Data S1.

DURHAM WINTER PREPARATION
There is no existing Durham winter final CSV, so the R script prepares this
dataset directly from Durham-1968-2023-winter.xlsx - DURCBC.csv in memory.
Headers are in row 4, annual counts in columns E:BG, and bird records in rows
6:183 (blank species-name rows omitted). Later summary, effort, weather, formula,
duplicate recent-year, and statistical columns/rows are not bird observations.
Blank annual count cells become zero, consistent with the other cleaned winter
datasets. * and x markers remain unknown counts. The main species list and
additional unidentified/hybrid records are retained for classification/audit.
The original large counts, including 1,000,000 Red-winged Blackbirds in 1971,
are preserved without adjustment; these outliers strongly affect annual totals.

MISSING COUNTS AND EXCLUSIONS
Numeric zeros are preserved. NA, N/A, blank values in existing finals, *, ?, and
x are not invented as numeric counts. Partial sums use available numeric values.
If all assigned species in a habitat/year are missing, the result is NA; if no
species are assigned to that group, it is zero.
The audit's habitat_coverage records numeric/missing/marked species counts for
every habitat/year, so incomplete sums can be identified. Twenty original
marker cells are recorded in non_numeric_records.
Existing Chapel Hill and Jordan Lake cleaning already converted some blank and
count-week values to zero; the habitat outputs preserve those prior decisions.

48 unresolved labels, including unidentified taxa and Rufous/Allen's Hummingbird,
are excluded from habitat sums. Unknown identity is not evidence of habitat
generalism. Their 814,990 numeric observations across files/years are retained
in the audit, including large Durham winter unidentified-blackbird flocks.
Party Species and RARER SPECIES are non-species rows; their 14,315 numeric
entries are excluded and recorded separately. No seventh habitat is invented.

VALIDATION
habitat_data_audit.json records source checksums, preparation steps, original
excluded labels and annual values, nonnumeric markers, missing-data coverage,
and annual reconciliations. All 356 source-year totals reconcile:
  habitat numeric sum + excluded numeric sum = input numeric sum.
Across all files and years, 6,361,905 numeric observations are assigned to
habitats. These are repeated survey observations, not distinct individual birds.
Every exported CSV is reread and checked against the R calculation.
